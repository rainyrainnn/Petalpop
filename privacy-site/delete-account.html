🌸 PetalPop Account Deletion

This page explains how PetalPop users can request deletion of their accounts and associated personal data.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the PetalPop app.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Open Settings.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Select Delete Permanently and confirm your request.

Unable to Access Your Account?

If you cannot access PetalPop, email us at reynaannjoybausin@gmail.com with the subject "PetalPop Account Deletion Request".

Include the email address associated with your PetalPop account. We may ask you to verify account ownership before processing the request.

What Information Is Deleted?

When account deletion is successfully completed, PetalPop is designed to delete:

  • Your Supabase authentication account.
  • Associated account records and app data stored in the database.
  • Uploaded photos and voice recordings associated with your account.
  • Locally stored account-related information on the device where deletion is performed.

Data Retention

We do not currently specify an additional retention period for account data after successful deletion. Any information that must be retained for legal or security reasons will be handled as required by applicable law.
