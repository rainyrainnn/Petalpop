🌸 PetalPop Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

PetalPop ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, where it is stored, and how you can request its deletion.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: Email address and authentication information used to create and access your account.
  • Profile information: Profile name, avatar, and selected preferences.
  • Mood check-ins: Selected moods, emojis, notes, and associated dates or times.
  • Memories: Entries, notes, photos, and voice recordings you choose to save.
  • App activity: Daily challenge completions, garden progress, flower unlocks, and related activity.
  • Notifications: In-app notifications and related preferences.
  • Device-stored information: Certain preferences, cached information, and entries stored locally on your device.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use this information to manage your account, provide mood check-ins and memory features, track challenges and garden progress, store your uploaded content, provide notifications, maintain app functionality, and respond to support requests.

3. Where Your Information Is Stored

PetalPop uses Supabase for account authentication, database services, and private file storage. Account-related information may be stored in our Supabase project. Photos and voice recordings are stored in private storage with access restrictions. Certain information may also be stored locally on your device.

4. Sharing of Information

PetalPop does not sell your personal information. Supabase processes information as our service provider for authentication, database, and storage services. We may disclose information when required by law or when necessary to protect the security and operation of the service.

5. Data Retention

We retain account information and associated app data while your account is active or as needed to provide the app's features. You can request deletion through the app.

6. Account and Data Deletion

You can delete your account through Settings → Delete Account → Delete Permanently.

Account deletion is intended to remove your Supabase authentication account, associated account records, and uploaded files. The app also removes locally stored account-related information from the device where deletion is performed.

If you cannot access the app, contact us at the email address below to request account deletion. Please include the email address associated with your account. We may need to verify ownership before processing a request.

7. Data Security

We use available security measures, including authenticated access and private storage restrictions, to help protect your information. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

PetalPop is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws. Please contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our features or data practices change. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
